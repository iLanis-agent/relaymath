Relay Math

Your watch says pace. The finish line says time. Somebody has to do the multiplying.

Boundary: arithmetic only Relay Math turns the times and distances you type into paces and totals. It never says what you should run, how you should train, or whether you are ready for a race.

The pace truth

10 km in 52:30 is 5:15 per km, 8:27 per mile. Both units, rounded once to the second.

The finish truth

A 5:15 per km pace over 10 km finishes in 52:30. Run it forward to see the clock you are chasing.

The relay truth

4 legs: 12:10 + 11:45 + 13:02 + 12:30 = 49:27 - slowest 13:02, fastest 11:45, a gap of 1:17. The team total is just the legs added up.

Run your numbers
Relay Math is exact arithmetic in whole seconds over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
